        Questions

        Before you order

        Still unsure? Send a photo of the room and we will match the paper to it.

        How many books should I choose?

        Two reads as a gift, three is our default and the most balanced fan, five and up looks like a library bouquet. A single book with a heavy ring of blooms is a favourite for anniversaries.

        Can I send my own books?

        Yes. Choose Your picks in the Reads section, send the titles, then drop them at the counter or post them to us. We subtract the book cost from the total.

        Are the flowers real?

        Fresh blooms, and we swap to whatever the market has that week, keeping the colour you picked. If you need it kept forever, ask for the dried version.

        How far ahead should I order?

        Three days for the standard counts. Allow a week if you want a specific title or a colour we have to source in.

        Do you deliver?

        Local delivery within the city, and pickup from the counter is free. Larger orders travel by courier, priced at checkout.

        Why does the price differ from the estimate?

        The estimate covers books, blooms, paper, and extras. It shifts slightly when a flower is out of season or you swap in a hardback.
